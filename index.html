How the game works
This is a demonstration, not an analysis. The Wattson Building is a dummy building and every number here comes from the simple mock model below, not from EnergyPlus.

Baseline is the building's current operation: HVAC Operation 6:30AM to 8:00PM every day, occupied 8:00AM to 5:00PM on weekdays, unoccupied at weekends. Every figure on the page is the change against that.
Setback is whatever sits outside operating hours, counted as 5 weekdays and 2 weekend days a week. Occupied time outside operating hours costs comfort. Operating time beyond occupancy is waste.
Baseline 240,000 ft² · 2,400,000 kWh/yr HVAC electricity · $0.21/kWh · 0.24 kgCO₂e/kWh.
Night purge +1.4% energy, or +1.7% with glazing, and lowers morning CO₂. Ventilation boost −1.8% energy, or −0.7% with heat recovery; much better CO₂, worse PM2.5 unless filtration is good. Glazing +6.2% / $1.85M, steadies temperature but the tighter envelope cuts background air so CO₂ rises. Heat recovery +4.4% / $920k and adds air cheaply. Filters cost fan energy as they get finer and set the PM2.5 floor.
Air changes drive CO₂: background air through the envelope plus whatever the systems supply. That is why glazing and ventilation pull in opposite directions.
Temperature drifts a little through the envelope even when the hours are right, which is what glazing steadies, and drifts more the longer the setback runs. Push setback too hard and temperature slips out of the health-optimized band.
Ranges are ±13% around the central estimate, standing in for modelling and weather uncertainty.
Results only change when you submit, so each play is a round rather than a live readout.
